voices at capacity:
  a chain is tried in order: when VoxCPM2 is at capacity (every instance at its limit) or down, the sentence waits briefly (VOXCPM_QUEUE_S, 2 s) for a free instance, then goes to the next voice (languages with no voice after VoxCPM2 wait up to VOXCPM_WAIT_S, 15 s); eSpeak NG (last resort) ends every chain it speaks, if espeak-ng is installed
  VoxCPM2 speaks 10 languages (ar, id, km, ko, lo, ru, sw, tl, tr, vi); one instance makes ~2-3 real-time voices, and takes up to 1 sentences at once (VOXCPM_MAX_INFLIGHT)
  VoxCPM2 instances: auto (the commercial default): one on the main GPU plus one on each other GPU with ≥ 10 GB, up to 4 — depends on the GPUs at start (VOXCPM_INSTANCES, VOXCPM_GPUS)
