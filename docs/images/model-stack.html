Measured: the real server with everything except VoxCPM2 used 24.8 GB ready and 26 GB serving four rooms. With VoxCPM2 (+6.2 GB measured) ≈ 31–32 GB: at the limit of a 32 GB card. The 24 GB figure (~21 GB) is an estimate. ASR = Whisper large-v3; Omni = Omnilingual; 3B = MADLAD 3B; other = VoxLingua, Kokoro, Coqui/MMS and CUDA working memory. Dashed line = 24 GB.

What the 24 GB card costs you

  • No VoxCPM2. Commercial: Khmer and Lao become text-only. Non-profit: back to MMS voices (Khmer recognised at 22.8% error instead of 8.5%).
  • MADLAD 3B instead of 7B for languages outside Hy-MT2: Haitian Creole 81% → 67%, English→Lao 74% → 54%, more critical errors.
  • Unchanged: recognition, and translation for Hy-MT2's 36 languages (the bulk of use): 95%.
  • Fewer simultaneous rooms before latency rises (less headroom).

Alternative 24 GB trade: keep MADLAD 7B and drop Omnilingual; that loses speech input for Khmer, Lao, Haitian Creole and Swahili entirely, so it is not recommended.
