# Test clips

Two short clips for `tools/smoke.py` (GPU, a running stack) and
`tests/test_protocol.py` (CPU, stand-in models). 24 kHz mono PCM16 WAV, the
stack's input format.

| File | Language | Says | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| `en.wav` | English | Could you tell me where the train station is? | 3.1 s |
| `km.wav` | Khmer | កុំបារម្ភអី បន្តិចទៀតប្រហែលម្ភៃនាទីគ្រូពេទ្យនឹងជួបហើយ។ ("Don't worry, the doctor will see you in about twenty minutes.") | 4.9 s |
| `es_long.wav` | Spanish | Five sentences of church announcements, silences trimmed so no quiet stretch exceeds 160 ms (past 5 s of speech the stack ends an utterance on 280 ms): the room's forced sentence cut has to do the splitting (`tools/smoke.py`, `es_long`) | 19.2 s |

`en.wav` and `km.wav` are **synthetic voices generated by this stack itself** (its own
text-to-speech, during the September 2026 model evaluation: the
`stack_en_00` and `stack_km_06` samples), not recordings of a person and not
taken from any dataset, so they carry no third-party licence and no personal
data. `km.wav` was trimmed of leading and trailing silence; it is larger than
the English clip because the sentence is longer.

Synthetic speech is easier than a real speaker in a real room (see
docs/dev/lessons-learned.md, "Synthetic tests mislead"): these clips prove the
pipeline works, not how well it hears people.

`es_long.wav` was generated with the stack's Kokoro voice `ef_dora` (Kokoro-82M, Apache 2.0) on 2026-09-29: synthetic, no third-party recording or personal data.
